Privacy Policy

App: Speaker Cleaner: Water & Dust (package com.flankerc.speakercleaner)
Developer: FlankerC
Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the app Speaker Cleaner: Water & Dust ("the app") may process. The app has no account, no login and no server operated by the developer. The developer does not directly collect, store or sell your personal information. The app uses a few Google services, listed below, that may process some data on your device as described.

1. What the app does on your device

  • It generates sound tones on your device and plays them through the speaker. No audio files are downloaded.
  • It stores your settings (selected language, your answer to the "what is wrong with your speaker" question, and whether onboarding was completed) only on your device.
  • It does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or files.

2. Third-party services used by the app

The app includes the following Google services. They may collect data automatically. Their own privacy policies apply to that data.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) - shows ads. May process the Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and ad interaction data, in order to serve, measure and protect ads. See Google Advertising policies.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) - shows a consent form where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) and records your consent choice on your device. See Google consent help.
  • Firebase Analytics - collects usage events (for example screens opened and the cleaning modes used), device and app information, the app instance ID, and the Advertising ID where permitted, to help the developer understand and improve the app. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics - collects crash reports and diagnostic information (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, crash time, an installation identifier) to fix crashes.
  • Firebase Remote Config - downloads configuration values (for example switching an ad placement on or off). The service receives the app instance ID, device and app information to deliver the configuration.

Types of data these services may process: Advertising ID, device identifiers and device information, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), app usage events, advertising interaction data and crash logs. The developer uses this data only to show ads, measure and improve the app, and fix crashes.

3. Your choices

  • Consent: where required, you can accept or decline ad personalization in the consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options (shown when required in your region).
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path may vary by device).
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app removes the settings stored on your device.

4. Data sharing and retention

The developer does not sell your personal data. Data collected by the Google services above is shared with and retained by Google according to Google's policies. The app does not send any data to other third parties.

5. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. It is not intended for children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact the developer.

6. Security

The app does not run its own servers. Data transmitted by Google services is sent over secure connections.
